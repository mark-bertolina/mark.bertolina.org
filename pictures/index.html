Astronomy                     
Analyzing images is one of my hobbies. I love observing things because there are always new ways of seeing the same thing, and then other new things to see all together. In other words an unchanging object will look different to you each time you observe it. This is because humans don't record a picture like a movie camera they have an active experience called seeing. Pictures give people a chance to concentrate on a single image. You can look at it, look at a piece of it, look at it upside down, and most importantly come back to it later. So anyhow these are images I created that catch my eye, and their really just here because of that.
