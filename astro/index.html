 Hubble Data Archive - This is a huge site with tons of astronomy information including:

       The STScI Digitized Sky Survey - This allows you to download a picture from any part the Sky Survey. The Palomar Observatory Sky Survey is a series of pictures taken by the Oschiny Schmidt Telescope on Palomar Mountain and the UK Schmidt Telescope covering most of the northern hemisphere.
       Hubble Space Telescope Public Pictures - An archive of all of the public images and text releases.


Bill Arnett's WWW Documents - This guy has written many informative HTML documents on Astronomy. They Include:

 The Nine Planets is a multimedia tour of our solar system's planets, moons, comets, and astroids.
   The Web Nebulae has pictures of a couple dozen of the more spectacular nebulae in the night sky.
    The Messier Objects contains a picture of each Messier object from the The STScI Digitized Sky Survey.
